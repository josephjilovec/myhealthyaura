04 / Methodology

The approach

Three methods are integrated into a single individualized framework. They are not packaged as disconnected services or delivered from a fixed script.

01

Targeted conversation

Structured, purposeful dialogue surfaces recurring patterns, clarifies the goal, and identifies where intention breaks down in daily life.

02

Behavioral conditioning

Practical, repeatable frameworks help change the cues, responses, and reinforcement patterns that shape everyday follow-through between sessions.

03

Individualized hypnosis

NGH-certified hypnosis protocols may be integrated to support attention, pattern disruption, and reinforcement at a deeper level when suitable for the participant’s goals.

Why individualized, not programmatic

A standard curriculum assumes that similar goals have similar causes. In practice, two people can struggle with the same outward behavior for completely different reasons. Sessions are therefore structured around the participant’s patterns, constraints, and definition of progress, with the method adjusted as useful information emerges.
